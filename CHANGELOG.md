# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-09-28)

First public release.

- Import bookmarks from Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Opera, Firefox, or a bookmark file.
- AI summaries, categories and tags through Ollama or OpenRouter.
- Search, collections, favorites, and grid, graph, timeline and summary views.
- Ask your library, and combined reports from selected bookmarks.
- Link health checks, page previews, full backup and restore.
- `mynk-mcp`: agents can search and add bookmarks over MCP.
- English and Turkish, light and dark themes.
- In-app update check; updates are verified against a signature before they install.
